✦ OUR STORY

About ThreeLittleWonders

Learn • Explore • Grow. ThreeLittleWonders is a child-friendly learning website offering introductory lessons, quizzes and guided activities from Nursery to Class 10.

Parents, caregivers and educators can explore age-appropriate learning roadmaps. Our current content is a growing set of starter resources, not a complete board-approved textbook syllabus.

✦ YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

Learning progress and activity practice are saved locally in your browser. They are not synced to an account or to our own database. Clearing browser storage may remove progress.

The contact form prepares a message in your email application. It does not send or store your enquiry on this website. Any email you choose to send is handled by your email provider and the receiving mailbox.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical request information to deliver the site. Please provide only a parent or guardian’s details when contacting us, and avoid sharing children’s private information.

For questions, email hello@threelittlewonders.com.

✦ USING OUR WEBSITE

Terms & Conditions

ThreeLittleWonders provides introductory educational resources for general learning and practice. Topics and examples may differ from a child’s school or board curriculum and should be checked against official school materials.

Adult guidance is recommended for younger learners, practical activities and use of external websites. We may revise or add educational content as the website develops.

The website currently has no online payment, student account or direct messaging service. Contact us for questions or corrections using the Contact page.

✦ CHILD FIRST

Child Safety

Young children should use online learning resources with a parent, guardian or teacher. Practical activities should use safe materials and appropriate adult supervision.

Do not post or send a child’s full name, school, address, private photographs, passwords or other identifying information. The enquiry form asks for a parent or guardian’s name and contact details.

External services, including email apps and any future social links, may have their own age requirements and privacy policies. Adults should review these before use.

✦ COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Which classes are included?

Nursery, LKG, UKG and Classes 1 to 10 have starter learning material. The topic roadmaps offer guided practice rather than full school textbooks.

Is learning progress saved?

Yes. Progress is saved in your current browser on this device, without an account. Clearing browser storage can remove it.

How do I send a message?

Open Contact, complete the enquiry form and press Send Message. Your email app opens a prepared draft that you must send yourself.

Are there downloadable worksheets and live classes?

The current site focuses on starter lessons and guided activities. Additional resources can be introduced as they are developed.
